Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 21, 2026

1. Core Privacy Commitment

SwiftConvert values your privacy. Our architecture ensures: Your original files (PDFs, images, etc.) are never uploaded to our servers. All processing occurs within your browser's local sandbox.

2. Data Collection & Usage

  • File Content: We do not access, store, or distribute your file data.
  • Access Logs: We collect anonymous info (e.g., browser type, IP address) solely for website traffic analysis.
  • Cookies: Used for language preferences and anonymous identifiers for Google AdSense/Analytics.

3. Third-Party Compliance

This site uses Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your interests. You can manage these preferences in Google Ads Settings.

4. Your Rights (GDPR)

We follow GDPR standards. Since data is processed locally, closing your browser tab instantly destroys all temporary traces in memory.
