What is HEIC?

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is the standard format used by Apple since iOS 11. While it saves 50% more space than JPG, it lacks compatibility with Windows and older Android devices. ToolHelper allows you to bridge this gap instantly.

Privacy & Local Processing

Unlike other online converters, our engine runs directly in your browser. Your photos are never uploaded to any server. This client-side approach ensures your private data stays on your device while eliminating upload wait times.

HEIC Conversion Technology

ToolHelper utilizes high-performance Libheif wrappers. When processing .heic files, the script identifies image sequences and extracts the primary frame. This process is pixel-accurate, preventing color shifts commonly found in lower-quality converters (such as Rec.709 to sRGB mapping issues).

Our tool is optimized for batch processing. Whether you're moving a dozen travel photos to your PC or preparing assets for social media, you can drop them all at once. The system leverages browser-based asynchronous tasks to convert files in parallel, maximizing efficiency.
