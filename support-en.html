Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Why did my conversion fail?

Since processing is local, failures usually occur if the file exceeds browser memory limits or if your browser lacks Wasm support. Chrome or Edge is recommended.

Q: Do I need to pay for this?

SwiftConvert is 100% free. We sustain operations through non-intrusive ads and never charge our users.
