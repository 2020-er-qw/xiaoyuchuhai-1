Why Convert WebP?

WebP is a format developed by Google to speed up web loading times. While highly efficient, it remains incompatible with many legacy office suites (like older PowerPoint versions), offline photo viewers, or certain social media platforms. ToolHelper bridges this gap seamlessly.

Ultimate Privacy

Our conversion logic is entirely based on the browser's HTML5 Canvas API. When you drag a file in, the browser simply redraws the image in memory and exports it. Your data never crosses the network. This is faster and more secure than any server-based online converter.

WebP Conversion Architecture

ToolHelper utilizes a high-performance asynchronous decoding scheme. By creating offscreen canvases, the system handles multiple WebP tasks without blocking the main thread's UI rendering. This approach perfectly preserves color profiles (ICC Profiles), ensuring the converted files look identical to the originals.

For WebP images with transparency, choosing JPG as the output will automatically fill transparent areas with a white background. Selecting PNG will maintain the full Alpha channel. This intelligent mechanism ensures all professional use cases are covered.
