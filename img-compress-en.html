ToolHelper: Privacy & Technical Commitment

1. Why Local Processing?

Most online tools upload your data to their servers, posing a massive privacy risk. ToolHelper brings the compression engine to your browser via WebAssembly. All calculations happen on your local hardware, and your files never leave your computer.

2. Pro-Grade Algorithms

Our tool integrates advanced quantization and sub-sampling techniques. It doesn't just "lower resolution"—it analyzes image structures to remove redundancy while preserving visual fidelity. Perfect for improving website speed and SEO scores.
